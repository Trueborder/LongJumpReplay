# Legal data needed before production publication

The legal pages are written with explicit placeholders. Replace each value below after confirming it with the seller, accountant, or lawyer. Do not publish unresolved square-bracket placeholders.

- `[[CONTROLLER_LEGAL_NAME]]`: full legal name of the controller/seller.
- `[[BUSINESS_FORM]]`: natural person, sole trader, company, or other legal form.
- `[[ICO]]`: Czech company identification number, if assigned.
- `[[VAT_ID]]`: VAT identification number, or state that the seller is not a VAT payer.
- `[[REGISTER_AND_FILE]]`: trade/register name and file number, if applicable.
- `[[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]]`: registered office or place of business.
- `[[CONTACT_ADDRESS]]`: address for complaints and exercising data rights, if different.
- `[[LEGAL_EMAIL]]`: legal and privacy contact email.
- `[[LEGAL_PHONE]]`: optional telephone contact.
- `[[SELLER_COUNTRY]]`: country of establishment and applicable supervisory authority details.
- `[[VAT_TREATMENT]]`: whether listed prices include Czech VAT, OSS VAT, or another verified treatment.
- `[[VAT_EVIDENCE]]`: accounting basis used to determine consumer VAT location and rate.
- `[[REFUND_ADDRESS]]`: address or email for withdrawal/refund requests.
- `[[MINIMUM_AGE_RULE]]`: confirmed age rule and parental-consent process.
- `[[SUPPORT_SLA]]`: realistic support and delivery-response period.
- `[[TERMS_EFFECTIVE_DATE]]`: date on which the published terms take effect.
- `[[RETENTION_SCHEDULE]]`: approved retention table for each data category.
- `[[HOSTING_PROVIDER_DETAILS]]`: current hosting/subprocessor name, location, and DPA link.
- `[[EMAIL_PROVIDER_DETAILS]]`: current transactional email provider and transfer safeguards.
- `[[MINECRAFT_SERVER_OPERATOR]]`: legal/operator identity for the Pantheon server if different from the seller.
- `[[LONGJUMPREPLAY_LICENSE_SCOPE]]`: final licence grant, territory, term, and permitted seats.
- `[[ACCESSIBILITY_CONTACT]]`: accessibility feedback contact and verified statement date.
- `[[ACCESSIBILITY_REVIEW_DATE]]`: date and method of the last accessibility review.

Before launch, verify the final terms, withdrawal wording, VAT treatment, age policy, Minecraft monetisation model, retention periods, and accessibility statement with a Czech/EU lawyer and accountant. This file is intentionally outside the public `tomaspisar.cz` asset directory.

## LongJumpReplay-only launch inputs

The following values are required specifically before the LongJumpReplay catalogue and checkout can be presented as complete legal information:

- `[[LJR_UPDATE_POLICY]]`: which software updates are included with lifetime and subscription licences, and for how long.
- `[[LJR_SUPPORT_SLA]]`: the actual support channel and realistic response/repair commitments.
- `[[LJR_PRICE_TAX_TREATMENT]]`: verified consumer price/VAT wording for each sales territory and whether VAT is included in displayed prices.
- `[[LONGJUMPREPLAY_LICENSE_SCOPE]]`: final personal, organisational, territory, seat and transfer scope.
- Confirm whether the 72-hour trial remains current and document its actual local-only behaviour.
- Confirm the seller's age/capacity and minimum-age or parental-consent rule for LongJumpReplay.
- Confirm whether the service is within the Czech implementation of the European Accessibility Act and provide `[[ACCESSIBILITY_CONTACT]]` and `[[ACCESSIBILITY_REVIEW_DATE]]` if applicable.
- Confirm the exact legal treatment of immediate digital activation and withdrawal, including when performance is considered complete.
- Confirm the retention schedule for orders, consent evidence, Stripe references, support correspondence, security logs and licence records.

The LongJumpReplay-specific pages intentionally retain these markers until the values are supplied. Do not describe the product as legally complete while any of these values remains unresolved.
