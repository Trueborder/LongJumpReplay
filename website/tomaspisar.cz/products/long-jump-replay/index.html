REQUIREMENTS

Ready for a Windows station.

LongJumpReplay is designed for Windows. A compatible camera is required for live capture.

  • Operating systemWindows 11, 64-bit
  • ProcessorRecent Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or better
  • Memory16 GB RAM
  • Storage & displaySSD with at least 10 GB free and a Full HD (1920 × 1080) display
  • CameraUSB 3 camera capable of 1280 × 720 at 60 FPS; 120 FPS is recommended for detailed review

For competitions, use mains power, disable Windows sleep, and test the complete computer, camera, cable, lighting, and recording mode before the event.
