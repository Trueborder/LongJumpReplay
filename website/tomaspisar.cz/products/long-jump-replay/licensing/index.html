Licensing and activation.

LongJumpReplay Professional comes two ways: a lifetime licence you buy once, or a monthly subscription. Both are activated with the email address you buy with, and both work identically. This page explains exactly what each covers, so nothing about the licence is a surprise after you pay.

01 / ACTIVATION

How activation works

Activation uses your email address, not a license key you have to keep safe. Five steps, and only the first three need an internet connection.

Purchase→ Email→ Verification→ Device activation→ Use LongJumpReplay

  1. Purchase

    Buy LongJumpReplay securely through Stripe. You enter an email address during checkout, and your license is created against that address once payment succeeds.

  2. Enter your email

    Install LongJumpReplay and start it. In the activation window, enter the same email address you used at checkout.

  3. Verify your email

    A verification code is sent to that address. Type the code into the application. This is what proves the license is yours, which is why you need an address you can actually open.

  4. Activate this computer

    After the code is accepted, this computer is added to your license and counts towards your device limit. Repeat on your second computer whenever you need it.

  5. Start using it

    LongJumpReplay is ready for competition use. From here on it works offline, and only reconnects occasionally to confirm the license is still active.

Use an email address you can access. It is how you activate, how you verify a new computer, and how we reach you about your license. Knowing the address alone is not enough to activate - the verification code has to be received and entered.

04 / AT THE TRACK

Internet and offline use

LongJumpReplay is built for athletics competitions, where connectivity is often poor or absent. It does not need a permanent internet connection while you are judging attempts.

An internet connection is needed twice: once to activate a computer, and then occasionally so the application can confirm your license is still active. A successful check refreshes the authorization stored locally.

LongJumpReplay can be used offline for up to 30 days after the last successful license verification. Losing connectivity for a weekend, or a whole competition season's worth of away meets, does not interrupt your work.

Connect the computer to the internet occasionally - at the club or at home is enough. If the offline period runs out before the next successful check, the application asks you to reconnect before you can carry on.

05 / COMPUTERS

Changing computers

Replacing a computer does not mean losing your license. Deactivate the old one, activate the new one.

Your license covers 2 activated computers at the same time. A typical setup is one office or club computer for preparing competitions, and one laptop taken to meets.

When you replace a machine, deactivate it so the slot is freed, then activate the replacement with the same email address. If you have already reached the device limit, activating another computer is refused until you free a slot.

If the old computer is broken, lost, or already wiped, sign in to the customer portal to review your devices. If you cannot access the purchase email, contact support.

06 / LICENSE STATUS

What your license status means

Almost every license stays Active for its whole life. The others are here so you know what a message means if you ever see one.

  • ActiveThe normal state. Your license is valid and can be activated and verified as usual.
  • InactiveThe license exists but is not currently activated on any computer - usually right after purchase, or after you deactivate every machine.
  • ExpiredApplies to the updates and support period, not to the software. Your installed version keeps working; you simply stop receiving new versions until you renew.
  • SuspendedRare, and only after something like a refund or a chargeback. If you see this and do not know why, write to us and we will sort it out.

The limits, stated plainly. You should not discover any of these after paying.

  • You need a working email addressActivation depends on receiving a verification code, so a shared club address nobody reads, or an address you are about to lose, will cause problems later.
  • Not unlimited computersThe license covers 2 computers at a time. Running three stations at one meet needs a second license.
  • Internet is required sometimesNot for daily use, but activation needs a connection and so does each periodic check. A computer kept permanently offline beyond 30 days will eventually ask to reconnect.
  • A subscription stops if you stop payingOn the monthly plan the licence stays active while the subscription does. Cancel, and it stops at the end of the paid period. A lifetime licence is not affected by this.
  • Updates are included for a periodWith a lifetime licence, updates and support are included for 12 months, and the software keeps working afterwards. On the monthly plan they are included for as long as the subscription runs.

08 / QUESTIONS

Frequent questions

If your question is not here, write to us and we will answer it - and add it to this list.

What email should I use to activate LongJumpReplay?

Use the same email address you entered when purchasing LongJumpReplay through Stripe. You must be able to access this email, because a verification code is sent to it and you have to enter that code into the application.

If you bought with an address you cannot read - a colleague's, or one that no longer exists - write to us and we will move the license to an address that works.

Can I use LongJumpReplay on multiple computers?

Yes, on up to 2 computers at the same time. Most people activate one office or club computer and one laptop they take to competitions.

Both activations use the same email address. If you need more stations than that - two runways judged at once, for example - each additional station needs its own license. Write to us if you need several and we will sort it out.

What happens if I buy a new computer?

You keep your license. Deactivate the old computer to free its slot, then install LongJumpReplay on the new one and activate it with the same email address.

If the old machine is broken, lost or already wiped, sign in to the customer portal to review your devices. If you cannot access the purchase email, contact support.

What if I reach the device limit?

If 2 computers are already activated, activating a third is refused until you free a slot by deactivating one of them. Nothing is lost and nothing is charged - you simply cannot exceed the limit.

Does LongJumpReplay require internet?

Internet is required for initial activation and for periodic verification. It is not required for continuous use: the application is designed to keep working offline between successful license checks.

In practice, connecting the computer occasionally at the club or at home is enough. You do not need connectivity at the track.

Will it stop working if my competition venue has no internet?

No. After activation you can work offline for up to 30 days from the last successful license check, which covers a competition weekend or a run of away meets comfortably.

That period is not indefinite. If it runs out before the next successful check, the application asks you to reconnect. Connecting the laptop before you travel is the simple way to avoid this.

What happens if I reinstall Windows?

Reinstalling Windows removes the local authorization, so the computer will need activating again with your email address. If possible, deactivate the computer before you wipe it, so the slot is freed cleanly.

If you forgot, and the slot is still taken by the old installation, write to us and we will free it.

Can I transfer my license?

Moving a license between computers is built in: deactivate one, activate another, as often as you need within your device limit.

Transferring a license to a different person or a different email address is not something you can do yourself, since the license is tied to the purchase address. Write to us - for a club handing equipment to a new coach, this is routine.

Should I buy the lifetime licence or the monthly plan?

The software is identical, and both give you two computers. The lifetime licence costs more up front and then nothing ever again. The monthly plan is a smaller amount each month and stops whenever you cancel.

If you run competitions every season, the lifetime licence works out cheaper. If you want it for one season, or need to spread the cost, take the monthly plan.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You keep working until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that the licence goes inactive and LongJumpReplay asks you to renew or buy a lifetime licence. Cancel through the link in your Stripe receipt, or write to us.

A failed card payment does not cut you off immediately - there is a grace period, so a card expiring on a Friday cannot ruin a Saturday competition. Your recordings and competition data are never touched either way.

What happens after my updates and support period ends?

With a lifetime licence, nothing stops working. It includes updates and support for 12 months, and after that you can carry on using the version your licence covers, indefinitely. Only continued updates and support would require renewal.

On the monthly plan there is no separate updates period: updates and support are included for as long as the subscription runs.

How is my payment handled?

Payments are securely processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and we never see your card number. From Stripe we receive your name, email address, billing country and the amount paid, which we keep as accounting records.

The email address you use at checkout is the one your license is created against, which is why it matters that you can access it.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The installer includes a replay-only 72-hour evaluation with up to three standalone video exports. Camera capture, freeze and replay are available; competition setup, rosters, judging, results and evidence packages are disabled. It needs no email, account or internet connection.

Use it to confirm your camera, capture hardware and computer all work together before you spend anything.
