PRIVACY / LONGJUMPREPLAY

Privacy.

LongJumpReplay is desktop software that runs on your own computer. It does not create an account, does not require an email address and sends no usage telemetry. The 72-hour trial and paid activation both work entirely offline.

Your recordings stay on your computer

Video, attempt records, competition data, settings and logs are written to your user profile on the machine you run the application on. Nothing is uploaded and there is no cloud storage involved.

The machine code

To activate a purchased license, the application calculates a machine code from your operating system, computer name and network adapter identifier. It is calculated locally and is never transmitted automatically. It leaves your computer only if you email it to me yourself in order to obtain a license key.

The free trial is recorded on the computer it is started on, so that one computer cannot restart the trial repeatedly. That record stays on your computer as well.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe, which acts as the payment processor and receives your card details directly. I never see your card number. From Stripe I receive your name, email address, billing country and the amount paid, which I keep as accounting records for as long as Czech law requires.

Email correspondence

Emails you send me, including machine codes and the license keys I issue, are kept so that I can provide support and issue replacement keys after a hardware change. They are not used for marketing and are not sold. Ask me and I will delete them.

For anything about your data, write to info@tomaspisar.cz.

PLANNEDA planned licensing model would store an email address and a record of activated computers on a server. It is not built, and nothing described on this page changes until it is.
