PRIVACY / LONGJUMPREPLAY

Privacy.

LongJumpReplay is desktop software that runs on your own computer. It sends no usage telemetry and uploads none of your recordings. The 72-hour trial needs no email address and no network connection at all; a purchased license is activated and periodically verified online, which is the only thing described here that leaves your computer.

Your recordings stay on your computer

Video, attempt records, competition data, settings and logs are written to your user profile on the machine you run the application on. Nothing is uploaded and there is no cloud storage involved. License checks carry no information about competitions, athletes or recordings.

Licensing and activation data

When you buy a license, the email address you used at checkout is stored so the license can be issued and activated. Verification codes are sent to that address, and I use it to contact you about your license.

A record of the computers your license is activated on is also stored, so the device limit can be applied and so you can deactivate a computer and activate a different one. Each computer is identified by a value derived from that computer; it is not a name, a location, or anything that identifies you personally.

Periodic license checks confirm that a license is still active and record when the check happened. This data exists to run the licensing system, is not used for marketing, and is not sold.

For activation security and account support, each activation and periodic check records the exact IP address, country reported by the network, app version, Windows version and processor architecture. This activity history is visible in your customer portal and is automatically deleted after 365 days. When you delete a deactivated computer in the portal, its detailed activity history is deleted immediately; a minimal licence audit event is retained.

The free trial is different: it is recorded only on the computer it is started on, so that one computer cannot restart it repeatedly. Nothing about the trial is sent anywhere.

Cookies on this website

The first-party ljr-consent cookie records whether you accepted or declined optional preferences for 180 days. If you accept, site-theme and site-language remember your optional display preferences for one year. They are set by tomaspisar.cz, use Secure and SameSite=Lax on HTTPS, and contain no account or payment data.

If you decline, the optional theme and language cookies are deleted and those choices last only until you close the tab. You can change your decision at any time through Cookie settings in the footer. There are no analytics or advertising cookies. The LongJumpReplay purchase page loads Stripe's payment component; Stripe may set cookies necessary for payment and fraud prevention under its own retention periods. The account portal uses ljr-portal-session, an essential 30-day server-side session cookie set by api.tomaspisar.cz. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, so JavaScript cannot read it. Signing out revokes the server session and expires the cookie.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe, which acts as the payment processor and receives your card details directly. I never see your card number. From Stripe I receive your name, email address, billing country and the amount paid, which I keep as accounting records for as long as Czech law requires.

Email correspondence

Emails you send me are kept so that I can provide support, free a device slot when a computer is lost or broken, and answer licensing questions. They are not used for marketing and are not sold. Ask me and I will delete them.

Retention and your choices

Licensing records are kept for as long as the license is in use, and accounting records for as long as Czech law requires. You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for deletion of anything not required for accounting. For anything about your data, write to info@tomaspisar.cz.
