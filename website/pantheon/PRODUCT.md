## Users and purpose

The public site introduces the Czech Minecraft Java survival community and helps visitors join at `pantheon.tomaspisar.cz`. Returning players use its wiki, map, Discord, player account, and live event links. Czech and English routes are required. These facts are preserved from the existing site and README, rather than newly invented product claims.
